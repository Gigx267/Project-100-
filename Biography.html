Biography

Shetu Gamaliel is a dynamic Nigerian entrepreneur, educator, and social impact leader whose multifaceted career bridges culinary excellence, youth empowerment, and women's leadership development. Based in Abuja, she has built an inspiring legacy that combines business acumen with compassionate social impact, driven by her belief that sustainable change comes from equipping individuals with both skills and opportunities.

"True leadership is about creating pathways for others to succeed while building sustainable systems that outlive your personal involvement."

Culinary Entrepreneurship

As the founder of Dine and Pack Culinary School and Restaurant, Shetu has trained and mentored hundreds of aspiring chefs and food entrepreneurs. Her institution is renowned for its holistic approach that combines:

  • World-class culinary instruction with international best practices
  • Practical business training and financial literacy
  • Entrepreneurial mentorship and industry networking
  • Personal development and leadership coaching

This comprehensive model produces graduates who excel both in culinary arts and business management, creating ripple effects throughout their communities. Under her leadership, Dine and Pack has become one of Abuja's most respected culinary institutions.

Social Impact Through Project100

Shetu Gamaliel is the visionary behind Project100, a social impact initiative committed to empowering underprivileged youth and underserved communities through targeted interventions. Born out of a deep desire to give back meaningfully to society, Project100 has:

2,000+ Direct Beneficiaries

Impacted through various programs and initiatives

Vocational Training

Providing free skills acquisition and business startup support

Project100 has executed various large-scale outreach programs, including medical outreaches, food and essentials distribution, and empowerment drives across vulnerable communities. Key highlights include multiple outreach programs at the Zhidu community and internally displaced persons (IDP) camps, where participants benefited from both skill-building programs and critical relief items.

The initiative has also conducted community orientations to promote self-reliance, financial literacy, and mental wellness among youth and women. Project100 runs impactful campaigns such as:

  • Back-to-School Drive: Provides school supplies and educational support to disadvantaged children
  • Blessedfeet Campaign: In partnership with Bata Nigeria, this initiative aims to offer clothing, footwear, and dignity support to over 100,000+ vulnerable children across Nigeria

Leadership in Women's Empowerment

In her broader leadership capacity, Shetu currently serves as the President of AWCNext (Africa Women Conference Next), a platform that amplifies the voices, ideas, and innovations of African women across sectors. Through this role, she:

  • Builds collaborative ecosystems for young women leaders
  • Drives inclusion and diversity in entrepreneurship
  • Elevates conversations around development, empowerment, and policy change across Africa

Her consistent efforts in education and social impact recently earned her the prestigious role of United Nations Girls and Women Speak Ambassador. This appointment recognizes her voice and leadership in championing gender equity and youth empowerment.

Current Focus

Today, Shetu continues to mentor youth, advocate for women's inclusion in entrepreneurship, and volunteer her expertise in programs centered on skills development and nation building. Her current priorities include:

  • Expanding Project100's reach to more underserved communities across Nigeria
  • Developing scalable models for vocational education and entrepreneurship training
  • Strengthening partnerships with corporate and international organizations to amplify impact
  • Advocating for policy changes that support youth and women's economic empowerment

Legacy & Future Vision

Shetu Gamaliel represents a new generation of African leaders who are redefining social entrepreneurship. Her work demonstrates how business excellence and social impact can create powerful synergies that drive sustainable development.

Looking ahead, she envisions building an ecosystem where culinary arts, vocational training, and women's empowerment converge to create unprecedented opportunities for Nigeria's youth and underserved populations. Her ultimate goal is to establish models of empowerment that can be replicated across Africa, creating waves of positive change that uplift entire communities.
